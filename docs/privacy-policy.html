NotiFlow Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 29, 2026  |  Developer: Prem Raj  |  Package: com.premraj.notiflow

Privacy Commitment: NotiFlow is designed from the ground up as a private, on-device notification management inbox. All notification capture, categorization, priority scoring, smart actions, and search processing happen locally on your device. We do not sell your personal data, and we do not transmit your notification content to any cloud AI or third-party servers.

1. Data NotiFlow Can Access

When you explicitly grant Android Notification Access (NotificationListenerService), NotiFlow can observe incoming notifications from enabled applications on your device. Depending on the applications you have installed, this can include:

  • Source application name and package identifier;
  • Notification titles, message text, and timestamp details;
  • Sender and conversation participant information exposed by Android;
  • Verification codes (OTPs) contained in incoming alerts;
  • Transaction/banking details (amount, merchant or party, account references, balance hints) contained in incoming financial SMS/push alerts.

We treat all captured notification content as strictly confidential and sensitive. It is never treated as anonymous or non-sensitive telemetry.

2. How Your Data Is Used

Captured notification content is used solely to provide user-requested on-device utility features:

  • Smart Inbox & History: Storing and presenting notification history so you never lose important alerts.
  • On-Device Rules & Categorization: Organizing notifications into OTP, Payments, Work, Personal, Updates, Promotions, and Spam using offline deterministic pattern matching.
  • Search & Reminders: Allowing you to query your past notifications and set reminders with approximate background delivery via Android WorkManager.
  • Optional OTP Clipboard Copying: When explicitly enabled by you in Settings, detecting OTP codes and copying them to your device clipboard with sensitive flag hints.
  • Optional Expense Tracking: When explicitly enabled by you in Settings, extracting transactional expense records for your personal financial dashboard.
  • Hands-Free Voice Reader: When enabled by you, announcing eligible alerts via on-device Text-to-Speech (excluding OTPs) when headphones are connected.

3. On-Device Storage & Security

All captured notifications and derived financial records are stored exclusively in an application-private SQLite database on your device (notiflow.db).

  • Android Sandboxing: Access to the database is restricted to the NotiFlow application sandbox.
  • No Cloud Backup: System cloud backup (android:allowBackup="false") is disabled to ensure notification data is not mirrored to external cloud backups.
  • Password-Protected Archives: When you choose to export your history, the archive is encrypted on-device using authenticated AES-GCM with a PBKDF2-derived key based on your personal passphrase. Archives are shared securely via Android's FileProvider.

4. No Cloud AI & No Automatic Model Downloads

NotiFlow's core classification and intelligence engine runs entirely offline using deterministic rules. NotiFlow does not connect to external LLM APIs, cloud inference providers, or background model download services.

5. Data Retention & User Controls

You have complete control over your data stored in NotiFlow:

  • Application Exclusion: You can exclude specific applications from ever being captured by NotiFlow.
  • Configurable Retention: You can configure automatic cleanup (7, 30, 90, or 180 days) for stale notifications. Pinned items and reminders are protected from automatic deletion. Financial transaction records remain decoupled from notification cleanup unless you explicitly delete them.
  • Delete All History: You can wipe all notification records and derived expense transactions at any time with a single tap in Settings.
  • Revoking Access: You can disable Notification Access at any time in Android system settings.

6. Third-Party Sharing & Data Sale

We do not sell, rent, monetize, or transmit your personal data or notification content to any advertisers, data brokers, or third parties.

7. Children's Privacy

NotiFlow is a general-purpose notification management tool and is not directed towards children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

If we update this Privacy Policy, the revised version will be published here with an updated effective date. Any material change in how data is accessed or handled will be accompanied by an in-app notice.
